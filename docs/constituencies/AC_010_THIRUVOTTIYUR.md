## 5. Electoral Political Economy Diagnostic

In AC #10, TVK secured a historic breakthrough by capturing 53.15% of the ballots. The victory was driven by strong youth mobilization in the 18 to 29 age bracket combined with substantial leakage from traditional AIADMK and anti-incumbent DMK voters. Despite substantial welfare disbursements under KMUT (79,744 female recipients), acute household dissatisfaction with TASMAC alcohol density (31 retail outlets) eroded the ruling party's advantage.
