## 5. Electoral Political Economy Diagnostic

In AC #224, TVK secured a historic breakthrough by capturing 34.72% of the ballots. The victory was driven by strong youth mobilization in the 18 to 29 age bracket combined with substantial leakage from traditional AIADMK and anti-incumbent DMK voters. Despite substantial welfare disbursements under KMUT (88,662 female recipients), acute household dissatisfaction with TASMAC alcohol density (29 retail outlets) eroded the ruling party's advantage.
