## 5. Electoral Political Economy Diagnostic

In AC #147, TVK secured a historic breakthrough by capturing 35.83% of the ballots. The victory was driven by strong youth mobilization in the 18 to 29 age bracket combined with substantial leakage from traditional AIADMK and anti-incumbent DMK voters. Despite substantial welfare disbursements under KMUT (112,382 female recipients), acute household dissatisfaction with TASMAC alcohol density (25 retail outlets) eroded the ruling party's advantage.
